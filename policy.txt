STORE POLICY

Return Policy

* Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving their order.
* Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original condition.
* Items should have their original tags attached.
* Items that have been worn, washed, or damaged by the customer may not be eligible for a return.

Refund Policy

* Customers can request a refund for eligible returned items.
* Refunds are processed after the returned item is received and inspected.
* Refund processing may take 5-7 business days after approval.
* Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the wrong or damaged item was sent.

Exchange Policy

* Customers can request an exchange within 7 days of delivery.
* Exchanges are subject to product availability.
* The item must be unused and in its original condition.

Shipping Policy

* Orders are usually delivered within 3-5 business days.
* Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location.
* Customers can ask the AI assistant to check the status of their order using their Order ID.

Order Support

* Customers can provide their Order ID to check order details and status.
* If an Order ID cannot be found, the customer will be informed that the order could not be located.
* Customers can request a refund through the AI support assistant by providing their Order ID and reason for the refund.

Customer Support

* The AI support assistant can answer questions about store policies.
* The AI assistant can look up orders using the Order ID.
* The AI assistant can create a refund ticket when a customer requests a refund.
* If the required information is unavailable, the AI assistant should clearly tell the customer rather than making up an answer.
